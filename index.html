VIRGO: A Visuomotor Reference-Conditioned Generalist for Whole-Body Humanoid Interaction
Jinxian Zhou, Lin Wu, Xiangyu Huang, Yifan Sun, Siyuan Li, Peiqi Yu, Ziyu Li, Changliu Liu†
2027, under review
project page

We present VIRGO, a visual sim-to-real framework that learns diverse whole-body humanoid interactions from human demonstrations without teleoperated robot training data. We distill state-based reinforcement learning experts into one reference-conditioned visuomotor diffusion policy through mixed behavior cloning and online DAgger. Guided by MoCap or video-derived references and egocentric visual feedback, the same frozen policy transfers zero-shot to a Unitree G1 for object interaction, sitting, and climbing.

Bi-Adapt: Few-shot Bimanual Adaptaion for Novel Categories of 3D Objects via Semantic Correspondence
Jinxian Zhou, Ruihai Wu, Yiwei Liu, Yiwen Hou, Xunzhe Zhou, Checheng Yu, Licheng Zhong, Lin Shao
ICRA 2026  International Conference on Robotics and Automation
🏆 Best Paper Award Finalist
project page / arXiv / code

We present Bi-Adapt, a novel framework designed for efficient learning of bimanual manipulation for novel categories. We propose this diffusion-model-based framework for generalizable bimanual manipulation across object categories for different complex tasks. We introduce a few-shot adaptation strategy following contact points selection to enhance bimanual collaboration efficiently.

DexReprise: Reference-Guided Generalist Dexterous Manipulation from Human Motion and Videos
Tianming Wei, Jinxian Zhou, Yufeng Tian, Siheng Xia, Zhecheng Yuan, Huazhe Xu†
2027, under review

From human references to robot dexterity. Given human motion or video references, DexReprise learns a reference-conditioned dexterous policy that transfers offline human demonstrations to robot execution.
InterRole: Late-Binding Interaction Evidence for Participant-Grounded Two-Person Motion Understanding
Lin Wu, Bowen Dang, Jinxian Zhou, Xiangyu Huang, Hengjin Zhu, Jianglin Lan†
2027, under review

Who did what? Models that fuse the two participants before the view is known (top) keep their descriptions when the streams are exchanged: they follow the slots, not the people. InterRole (bottom) binds evidence to the view only at the end (late binding), so its descriptions follow the people. Left: share of interactions where both people get the same description (P1=P2) and where the relation follows the swap.
